# VPN Setup

## Installing the client
Download the GlobalConnect client from the company software portal. Sign in with
your corporate email and password.

## Connecting on macOS
Open System Settings, approve the GlobalConnect network extension, then click Connect.
If you see error 809, restart the client and make sure UDP ports 500 and 4500 are open.

## Who to contact
Contractors must request VPN access through their manager before connecting.
